STUDENT CONTROL

Discovery is not commitment

Nothing is applied for automatically. Opportunities are suggestions; the student decides what to save, prepare, apply to or ignore.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The LMS should not end at “course complete.” It should help students cross the bridge into real work.

Opportunity matching uses the student's evidence to reveal fit and gaps. Application actions remain explicit, student-controlled and traceable.
