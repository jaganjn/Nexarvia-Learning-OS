STUDENT CORRECTION

Keep the model honest

The Twin can be wrong. Students can correct goals, confidence, preferences or context instead of being silently profiled.

PRIVACY

Student-owned by design

The Twin is a learning aid, not a permanent label. Students control what is shared with mentors, faculty or employers.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The system should understand the learner without defining the learner.

The Learning Twin is explainable, correctable and evidence-driven. It exists to choose better next actions—not to rank students.
